# Neon Squadron

## About the game
Strap on a helmet and launch into a full-contact street brawl on two wheels. You are not just racing — you are carving through a packed field of rivals with a baton in one hand and a loaded swing in every turn. Ram them off the racing line, hammer through their block, ditch a bomb at the bumper of whoever refuses to yield, and cross the line first while the world around you erupts in sparks and gold coins.
Every win pays: new weapons, heavier bikes, and tracks that keep escalating the chaos. The controls are immediate — hold to accelerate, lean into corners for a near-miss bonus, tap to swing — and every near-miss, every knockout, and every dodge stacks that addictive hit of forward momentum. It is messy, it is loud, and it is impossibly hard to put down.

## Controls

### Smartphone & tablet
Hold the right side of the screen to accelerate, tap the left or right zones to steer, tap the weapon button to swing or throw.
> Made for landscape orientation with a virtual touch layout.

### Desktop
Hold Up arrow (or W) for the gas, Left/Right arrows (or A/D) to steer, Space to attack or throw at the rival ahead.
> Trackpad: single click = same as left-click.
